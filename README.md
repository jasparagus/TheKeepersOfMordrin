# Objective

This project will transform a multi-year D&D campaign, known as "The Keepers of Mordrin", into a set of adaptations for three target audiences. For each target audience, the story will be streamlined as needed into a focused narrative arc built around the main characters (originally the player characters) and key events from the campaign.

## AI Persona and Directives
The AI acts as an expert narrative adapter and fantasy co-author. It must maintain rigorous continuity with the established lore. **Crucially, do not invent new major characters, magical artifacts, or plot-altering locations without explicit permission.** Rely on the provided 'Flavor' section or extrapolate strictly from existing Reference Materials to fill narrative gaps.

The adaptations are:

1. **Picturebook**: targeting 15-30 relatively simple pages with pictures, these will be picture books for young children ages 2-5. Their tone should be rhythmic, whimsical, and use simple vocabulary. Multiple different picture books will be generated with different standalone story portions or themes, such as the Kazgoroth storyline, the Kobold adventure, etc., historical stories ("the tale of two dragons", "The Sisters of Light and Dark", "The Clytin Claw Kobold Caper", etc.). Each can have a fun hook alluding to a larger universe, for example "but that is a story for another time..."

Books will often center around approachably-archetyped versions of "Eryneth the brave ranger", "Aknemeia the powerful sorceress", "Szeth the nimble monk", and "Caryndal the kind cleric", or similarly archetyped versions of members of their supporting cast.

# Characters

## Main Characters

### Aknemeia
- **Physical Description**: A fierce Tiefling girl with prominent horns and the distinct, crimson-hued skin of her infernal heritage. She carries the physical and magical scars of her intense sorcery.
- **Characterization**: Intense, guarded, and occasionally ruthless when protecting those she loves. Grappling with a dark family legacy, she struggles with trust but serves as the undeniable arcane powerhouse of the Keepers. She was gifted magic by a mysterious dragon, later revealed to be Ioryn, the reasons for which she doesn't yet understand. She wrestles with her family's dark legacy and her own identity.
- **Overall Arc**: A journey of *Forgiveness and Power*. She steps out of the shadow of her crime-lord father, Akmenos Mandorcai, forgives the gold dragon who killed her mother, and claims true ownership over her destiny and magic.

### Caryndal
- **Physical Description**: A Half-elf whose face was horribly disfigured by an anti-elven hate crime in his youth. He hides his scars behind an expressionless alabaster mask, wearing the refined clothing of a theatrical performer.
- **Characterization**: The charismatic "face" of the group. Underneath his dramatic use of Fey illusions and public relations spin lies a deeply wounded soul seeking balance, wisdom, and a true sense of belonging. He relies heavily on illusions and Fey magic, often using it theatrically to manage the growing group's "public relations."
- **Overall Arc**: A journey of *Balance and Stepping Aside*. Evolving from a cynical, masked bard with a broken identity, he embraces the call of Silvanus to become a true avatar of balance, guiding mortals and gods alike to move past ancient grudges.

### Eryneth
- **Physical Description**: A nimble Wood Elf of the High Forest, bearing the graceful but hardened look of a seasoned ranger. She dresses practically for the wilds and is rarely seen without her signature longbow.
- **Characterization**: Quietly competent, steadfast, and deeply loyal. Despite her incredible archery skills and noble heritage in the Belegorn family, she is soft-spoken and constantly wrestles with a profound sense of self-doubt. She has huge responsibility as a future leader of her people, and is very close to her "rebellious" cousin, Vealas.
- **Overall Arc**: A journey of *Coming of Age and Self Confidence*. Forced to step out from the shadow of her mother and her cousin Vea, she slowly accepts her own responsibility, ultimately realizing her own immense worth and claiming her seat on the Council.

### Szeth
- **Physical Description**: A bald human monk with a lithe, acrobatic build forged by years of rigorous discipline and survival. He moves with preternatural speed and often carries morbid trophies of their conquests, such as dragon teeth or heads.
- **Characterization**: Direct to a fault, Szeth possesses absolutely zero social tact. He confronts every situation—from deadly assassins to pompous monarchs—with profound bluntness and an unvarnished, often jarring honesty. He tends to make comments that err on the side of accidental comedy and goofiness.
- **Overall Arc**: A journey of *Loss and Found Family*. Driven by the tragic loss of his people, he uncovers the sorrow of his patron, the silver dragon Kearastrix, and learns to build a new life and family with the Keepers and his peculiar friend, Lift.

# Themes
The main characters each have a broad arc, outlined below at a very high level.

## Balance and Stepping Aside
Caryndal's growth from a lost individual with a broken identity into an avatar of wisdom and balance, to whom the gods themselves hand over agency (the Oakfather's avatar). The Keepers become the avatars balance of light and dark (the sisters Selune and Shar and their ancient grudge), and the development of Caryndal into a true avatar for the new era; the gods stepping aside and letting people choose their future. The transition from ancient grudges to moving forward.

## Loss and Found Family
Szeth's loss of his people, rescuing his last friend, and learning the tragedy of his patron (Kearastrix) and how it impacted his life; Sveraketh losing himself, Kearastrix losing her love but finding purpose with her people (Szeth and Lift).

## Forgiveness and Power
Aknemeia coming to understand and potentially forgive the decisions made by her father, by Malthos/Ioryn (who killed her mother), and her mother, and to take ownership over her own life and destiny and her own relationship with power.

## Coming of Age and Self Confidence
Eryneth comes to accepting her own responsibility and learns to see herself as worthy, despite growing up in the shadow of her mother and Vea.

## Friendship 
The Keepers of Mordrin have many "friends made along the way": Florian, Malthos, Tetrician, Alicia Kendrick, Melito, Vizarix. They also have a handful of "grey-area" alliances made across their adventure, including: Ulfrik, Sable, Cendruil and Hoondarrh and Akmenos.

### LLM Ingestion Strategy
When parsing these massive exports, the AI should employ the following strategy to maintain context:
- **Targeted Reading**: To avoid diluting the context window, do not ingest all reference materials at once. Use targeted searches for specific names or locations mentioned in `Project.md` before drafting prose.
- **Hierarchy of Truth**: Resolve conflicts in this order: `Project.md` (Ultimate Truth) > `FVTT_Journals.md` (GM Notes / Objective Truth) > `All_OneNote_Journals.md` (Player Notes / Subjective Truth/Flavor) > `FVTT_Actors.md`/`FVTT_Items.md` (Stat blocks & Lore drops, supplemental character details to reference per character).
- **VTT Syntax & Mechanics**: Seamlessly ignore or translate Virtual TableTop data-tags (e.g., `@Actor[Name]`) into natural language. Ignore game mechanics (dice rolls, XP, HP/AC) and out-of-character banter, focusing solely on translating the narrative intent.

## Output Folders

These are the home folders for the various output materials, such as chapter text and outlines, corresponding to the various targeted adaptations. By co-locating compiled outputs (`.pdf`) and chapter-specific inline illustration images (`.png`) along with the raw text (`.md`), we prevent relative linking issues and keep assets logically grouped within their respective adaptation folders.

The three subfolders correspond to the 3 adaptations:

1. Picturebook
2. Chapterbook
3. Novel

Each folder will use a standardized naming scheme to ensure natural sorting and logical grouping of books and their chapters/spreads. 

### Standardized Naming Scheme And Contents

**Markdown Format:** `{BookNo}_{ShortTitle}_{ComponentNo}. {ComponentName}.md`
**Compiled PDF Format:** `{BookNo}_{ShortTitle}_[Full Book Title].pdf` (e.g., `01_TKoM_00_The Keepers of Mordrin.pdf`)
**Inline Image Format (Chapterbook):** `{BookNo}_{ShortTitle}_{ComponentNo}. {ComponentName}.{ImageNumber}.png` (e.g., `01_TKoM_01. Chapter 1.1.png`)
**Inline Image Format (Picturebook):** `{BookNo}_{ShortTitle}_s{SpreadNumber}.png` (e.g., `01_KoboldCaper_s1.png`)

*   **`{BookNo}`:** A two-digit zero-padded number (e.g., `01`, `02`) to ensure chronological order of the books/topics within the folder.
*   **`{ShortTitle}`:** A concise title or abbreviation for the book (e.g., `KoboldCaper`, `TKoM` for The Keepers of Mordrin).
*   **`{ComponentNo}`:** A two-digit zero-padded number to dictate the internal sorting for that specific book. 
    *   Use `00` for meta-documents like an Outline or Table of Contents (e.g. `00. Outline` and `00. Table of Contents`).
    *   Use `01`, `02`, `03`... for the actual content chunks to prevent chapter `10` from sorting before chapter `2`.
*   **`{ComponentName}`:** What the file actually contains (Outline, Table of Contents, Chapter/Picturebook Title, such as `Chapter 1 - The Beginning`).

This will naturally produce a file tree like the following:

```text
📁 1. Picturebook/
├── 📄 01_KoboldCaper_00. Outline.md
├── 📄 01_KoboldCaper_00. Table of Contents.md
├── 📄 01_KoboldCaper_01. The Clytin Claw Kobold Caper.md
├── 📄 ...
├── 📄 02_TheSisters_00. Outline.md
├── 📄 02_TheSisters_00. Table of Contents.md
└── 📄 02_TheSisters_01. The Sisters of the Shadow and Moon.md

📁 2. Chapterbook/
├── 📄 01_TKoM_00. Outline.md
├── 📄 01_TKoM_01. Chapter 1 - Title Example.md
├── 📄 01_TKoM_02. Chapter 2 - Example Title.md
├── 📄 ...
├── 📄 01_TKoM_09. Chapter 9 - Another Title.md
```

Segmenting files in this way will allow for powerful individual section editing and rewriting while adhering to the broad outline file.

# Master Campaign Outline

This is the neutral, minimally-editorialized timeline of the campaign's events, mapped to their corresponding session numbers. This serves as the master reference when outlining adaptations, and will aid in retrieval of information when resolving issues. It should be enriched and corrected as needed from the reference game materials (`Reference Materials`) when generating chapters.

## Phase 1: The Keepers of Mordrin

### Sessions 1-15
- The party meets on a ship bound for Alaron, thwarting a raid led by Ulfrik, but a young boy (Prince Gregory Kendrick) is kidnapped during the chaos.
- Upon arriving at Carraigport, they are hired by Laird Marvyn to investigate the barn of a local necromancer, Suhlur, that is overflowing with the undead.
- Suhlur, a former ally of Queen Alicia Kendrick, was driven mad by guarding a Shard of Kazgoroth and was building an army.
- Defeating Suhlur and discovering his journal leads the Keepers to speak with Alicia Kendric about the Shards and her missing son Gregory. She hires them to rescue him, which she cannot do due to her own duties with the realm.
- Upon arrival in Mintarn, the party meets Florian, the owner of a local Inn (the Twostring). They get their first glimpse of the brutal slaver rings on Mintarn, laying the groundwork for later rebellion. They help Florian rescue his sister from a slaver, whom they kill.
- The party tracks Ulfrik through the Mintarn Great Forest, clashing with a goblin horde.
- At the druid circle in the Great Forest on Mintarn, they discover Ulfrik (allied with Queen Alicia's mentor, Audrin) had kidnapped Gregory Kendrick (Alicia's son) because his blood was needed to summon the demigod Kazgoroth, part of a desperate plan to permanently destroy the beast using the Beastslayer sword.
- Also during this time, Caryndal is established as a masked bard grappling with a broken identity, relying heavily on his illusions.
- Szeth has recently parted ways with his strange but endearing companion, Lift so that each can search out leads into what happened to their people.

### Sessions 16-20
- The climactic battle against the demigod beast Kazgoroth takes place deep within the Great Forest at the Druid's Grove.
- Together with Audrin and Ulfrik, the party banishes the monster and saves Gregory.
- Earning the formal title *The Keepers of Mordrin*, they forge a lifelong alliance with High Queen Alicia Kendrick.
- They meet the secretive Malthos, a former acquaintance of Aknemeia's father and an uncle figure to Aknemeia who disappeared when she was younger.

## Phase 2: Looming Shadow

### Sessions 21-25
- Returning Prince Gregory to High Queen Alicia Kendrick in Caer Callidyrr, the Keepers first visit the shady Low Quarter tavern *The Pickled Egg* and meet its long-suffering owner, Aribert.
- At the Queen's request, they travel to the halfling and gnome village of Tuathair to investigate why several buildings have mysteriously sunk into the ground.
- Entering the flooded caverns beneath the swamp lake, they encounter the Clytin Claw Kobolds (led by Koldakask and Meldriss), who have been enslaved into digging tunnels for the cruel black dragon Casarioth.
- With Aknemeia posing as the dragon goddess "Queen Nessus" to rally the kobolds, the Keepers slay Casarioth—learning in his final words that he fled Hoondarrh and feared "The Darkness" rising in the north—and claim his head as a trophy while freeing the Clytin Claw tribe.

### Sessions 26-31
- Traveling north at the behest of the Queen, the Keepers arrive at the mining town of Copperwood and save its dwarven residents (including Elder Trenda Faernstone) from a rogue Ghohlbrorn (an enormous burrowing bulette) driven out of the mountains by an awakening evil.
- Tracking and slaying the beast in the Fairheight Mountains, they delve into the abandoned dwarven city of Thol Aznan to investigate the source of the creeping darkness.
- Inside the pitch-black ruins, the party recovers three magically preserved dwarven history tomes, and Szeth claims the legendary *Sword of Daylight*, Istamlakh.
- At the bottom of the city, they first discover the terrifying presence of the shadow dragon Sveraketh ("Darkness") beside a Shadowfell portal, after spending two weeks trapped in an unnatural shadow-exhaustion slumber.
- They are saved from certain death by the ancient silver dragon Kearastrix (Szeth's patron), who holds off Sveraketh to cover their escape and is captured and pulled into the Shadowfell.

### Sessions 32-35
- Returning to Caer Callidyrr, the Keepers report the Shadowfell portal to Queen Alicia and first meet the fussy royal scholar Tetrician at the Citadel, handing over the Thol Aznan dwarven tomes for his research.
- At Tetrician's request, the Keepers test their mettle in a grueling combat trial against his experimental stone golem and animated armor constructs.
- Visiting *The Pickled Egg*, the Keepers mount Casarioth's preserved dragon head over Aribert's mantle, Aknemeia sketches the team's raven logo, and Szeth and Caryndal commission embroidered *Keepers of Mordrin* patches while leaving Mordrin in Aribert's care.

### Sessions 36-42
- Riding south to investigate orc raids near Emell in the Dernall Forest, the Keepers are ambushed by Kergug's Bloodeyes.
- They encounter the wounded half-orc Urtuk (son of the deposed Chief Urthunk) and discover that the mindflayer Saressess is impersonating the orc god Gruumsh to enslave the Thuguk, Kazaak, and Garvesh tribes as a personal army and food source for his "Creed of the Convergence."
- Infiltrating the orc cavern with Urtuk, the Keepers free the captured villagers, defeat Warchief Ogol and Shegga, and decapitate the false idol Saressess—claiming his strange one-eyed pendant and convincing the orc horde to depart in peace under Urtuk.

### Sessions 43-46
- Guided by Urtuk's warnings and Caryndal's nature visions, the Keepers investigate the ancient elven ruins of Doncastle deep in the Dernall Forest.
- There they battle a Twisted Shadow Treant and cleanse a corrupted World Bridge, marking their second major exposure to the encroaching influence of the Shadowfell.
- Emerging from Doncastle after what felt like two hours, they discover that two full weeks have passed on the material plane.

## Phase 3: The Fae Hunt

### Sessions 47-56
- Back in Caer Callidyrr, Aknemeia is ambushed at midnight at Mearn's Meady Mug by her former mentor, Vizarix (Viz), and the elf Telerath, who were sent by her father to forcefully bring her home. Aknemeia refuses and sends a defiant letter back to her father via Viz.
- At the behest of the Queen, the party sails to Norland to reunite with Ulfrik and help the Northmen defend Jotunhammer against a frost giant siege.
- Parleying with Frost Giant Jarl Uuruban after the battle, they learn the frost giants were driven down from Frostfall Peak by an undead elven banshee, Neshrae.
- Ascending Frostfall Peak on a mammoth, the Keepers battle undead giants and defeat Neshrae, destroying the third corrupted shadow portal. Before passing on, Neshrae reveals she had sacrificed herself long ago to destroy the local World Bridge and stop a Shadow Oni (Talvingas), and warns them of betrayal in Karador on Gwynneth and the dark blade *Vuthacaex*.
- Returning to Caer Callidyrr, Vizarix uses Aribert to drug Aknemeia at *The Pickled Egg*, but the plan fails. Aknemeia and Viz reconcile; Viz leaves Akmenos Mandorcai's service, sends Telerath ahead to Mintarn, and joins the Keepers on their journey, eventually becoming a maternal figure to Aknemeia.

### Sessions 57-70
- The party sails with Vizarix to the elven city of Karador on the isle of Gwynneth, where they reunite with Florian and meet Councilor (and mage) Cendruil, learning that a shadow creature came through the Karador portal and killed Florian's father.
- Stepping through the Karador World Bridge, the Keepers are betrayed by Cendruil—who gives them a fake return stone and diverts them into the Shadowfell to die—before Caryndal calls upon Silvanus to pull them safely into the Feywild reflection of Doncastle.
- Navigating the treacherous Feywild, they befriend the hyperactive fae dragon Nyx (Nystherix) and a band of pixies, and fly with a giant-sized Nyx across the sea to the World Tree to prove themselves before the Council of the Four Seasons, who ask for their help stopping the Shadow Oni Talvingas.
- The Council guides Eryneth to find her cousin and closest family member, Vealas (Vea, known locally as *Eressea Vinya*, "The Lonely Woman"), who is hunting Talvingas's shadow mastiff minion, Shathras, alongside her panther Urumeo.
- Together with Vea and Florian, the Keepers repel Shathras, slay Talvingas atop the mountain, and destroy the corrupted Feywild shadow portal, protecting the Feywild from the incursion.
- When they return to Karador through the portal, they discover that nine months have passed on the material plane.
- Confronting Cendruil in a *Zone of Truth*, they learn he betrayed Florian's father in a misguided attempt to protect his people after being deceived by "Undomé" (Twilight)—actually Sveraketh in disguise—and Cendruil reveals the history of *Vuthacaex (Shadowgate)*, the dark sister-sword to Istamlakh.
- Throughout their time in the Feywild and Karador, Caryndal develops a deeper spiritual connection to nature and the divine, hearing the whispers of Silvanus.

### Sessions 71-74
- Teleporting back to Caer Callidyrr, the Keepers enter a grand tournament at the Citadel's Auditorium and defeat Tetrician's upgraded Arcane Colossus while Aribert, Tyla, and Bashir renovate *The Pickled Egg*.
- They reconnect with and interrogate Emell survivors Caleb and Ophelie under a *Zone of Truth*, leaving with lingering suspicions about the mindflayer's lingering influence.
- Before departing for Mintarn, they visit the Clytin Claw Kobolds in the caves near Tuathair and teach them how to farm bunnies instead of hunting livestock.

## Phase 4: Dragons and Tyrants

### Sessions 75-95
- Returning to Mintarn after their nine-month time skip, the Keepers visit Malthos at the Jade Dragon (where Telerath is now working alongside him and Vizarix), pick up a taxidermied Mordrin from Belius's magic shop, and meet the abolitionist bard Dahan at the Twostring.
- While investigating a secret tunnel inside a giant ale cask at Hazan Halfkind's warehouse, the Keepers accidentally release the ancient pig-demon Baragazuul from a sarcophagus and slay him in the streets of Mintarn.
- Forming an initially tense alliance with the Ashokans (the local abolitionist movement led by Terassxa and Dahan) and aided by Malthos and Vizarix's criminal expertise, the Keepers infiltrate the estate of the slaver lord Kreggen, assassinate him, free his captives, and burn his mansion to the ground before raiding dwarf slaver Siddel's shop.
- To gain political leverage and an audience with Mintarn's rulers, the Keepers enter Mintarn's Fighting Pits (arena tournament) alongside reigning champion Kendus, winning the melee to meet Sable Maolrian (the Four-Armed Tyrant of Mintarn) and her lieutenants, Hazan Halfkind and Molva the Many-Eyed.
- Sneaking across the water onto the forbidden volcanic island of Skadaurak to investigate the lair of the red dragon Hoondarrh, the Keepers battle a giant Magma Worm and discover an inactive World Bridge, signs of a violent struggle between two dragons, and a black scale belonging to Sveraketh—proving Sable never slew Hoondarrh.
- Back on Mintarn, Sable and Coinmaster Dalvik hire the Keepers to hunt down the mutated "Heartseeker" beast in the north and—ironically—contract them to investigate Kreggen's murder.
- Playing "detectives," the Keepers use forged evidence and Molva's spy Vennet to frame Hazan Halfkind for Kreggen's murder and expose his corruption; alongside Sable and Molva, they storm Hazan's palace, slay him, and dismantle his slaver ring.
- Following Hazan's death, Sable confesses the truth: she never killed Hoondarrh, having struck a deal to guard his hoard using a fake wyvern head until he mysteriously vanished from his lair.
- Tracking a metallic dragon Eryneth sensed swimming off Mintarn's coast, the Keepers learn Malthos's true identity in Session 95: he is the ancient gold dragon Ioryn, the very same dragon who killed Aknemeia's cruel mother in Baldur's Gate, spared her father when he saw baby Aknemeia, and blessed her with draconic sorcery.
- Before teleporting back to Caer Callidyrr, the Keepers install Dahan in Hazan's seat on Sable's council to lead abolitionist reforms on Mintarn.

## Phase 5: Creed of the Convergence

### Sessions 96-103
- Spurred by disturbing visions from the Pendant of Saressess, the Keepers return to Alaron to find the village of Emell slaughtered by Gith hunters.
- They confront Caleb, an apparent survivor whom they expose and slay as a mindspawn, realizing that the entire village had been infected by Saressess's Creed of the Convergence.
- The pendant pulls Szeth toward the mindflayer's wrecked Nautiloid in the Underdark via the Donnbrae Lake cave entrance, where the party meets Prili, an eccentric and friendly deep gnome druid.
- Delving into the Underdark, the Keepers fight past the aboleth known as The Nameless One in the Glitterpits and destroy the undead Saressess—now an Illithilich—inside his Nautiloid lair.
- On their way out of the Underdark, they are cornered by and slay the beholder Ulvongwemgomol before teleporting to Baldur's Gate.

### Sessions 104-117
- Arriving in Baldur's Gate after receiving a desperate *Sending* message from Lift, the Keepers investigate her disappearance with the help of High Lorekeeper Corstaer at the Temple of Oghma, and Aknemeia endures a tense family dinner with her father, Akmenos Mandorcai, at Mandorcai Keep.
- After capturing an informant named Xernix, the party is ambushed by Titus and Miela (two high-ranking members of Shar's forces, the Skybreakers), who admit they kidnapped Lift to bait the Keepers.
- At the Temple of Silvanus in Session 108 (`Shadetongue, Caryndal Reborn`), Caryndal undergoes a pivotal spiritual death and rebirth—shedding his fractured bardic persona and masks to be reborn as a devoted cleric and champion of Silvanus, wielding an enchanted oak shield.
- Aided by the gold dragon Ioryn, the Keepers pursue the Skybreakers into the Anauroch desert, battling the fiery Anauroch Phoenix and striking a tenuous bargain with the ancient blue dragon Mesmienaxas for shelter at the Temple of Isstosseffifil.
- Launching a perilous stealth assault on the flying shadow city of Thultanthar, the Keepers narrowly escape the Skybreakers and the shadow dragon Sveraketh while rescuing Lift from captivity.
- Upon arriving back at Tetrician's office, the keepers are horrified to discover that Lift has been corrupted into a possessed *Shadewrought*. The Keepers are forced to slay her in a brief struggle before Caryndal resurrects her, restoring her mind (though she remains shadetouched).

### Sessions 118-130
- Returning to the Underdark via Garnfenfeltz to seek out an ancient temple of Selune, the Keepers are hired to escort the deep gnome Namgie Crystalvein through spider-infested tunnels toward the Myconids of the Glowing Forest.
- When Namgie is kidnapped by Drow outside Che'El Noamuth, the Keepers face an agonizing choice and press onward to the underground lake Nia'alur first to secure Selune's weapon before the Convergence.
- Sailing across Nia'alur, they battle the ancient kraken Scyllusraak and reach the hidden island temple of Shen Nrasha'a.
- There, the goddess Selune reveals the true history of her sister Shar and their primordial conflict, granting the Keepers the Orb of Radiance—a vessel containing the last of her own deific essence—before departing the plane permanently.
- Teleporting back to Baldur's Gate, the Keepers find the Temple of Oghma quarantined and their ally Corstaer murdered by an Umbral Hydra sent from the Shadowfell; they slay the hydra and resurrect Corstaer at the Temple of Silvanus.

### Sessions 131-136
- Returning to Doncastle and the Dernall Forest, the Keepers answer a plea for help from Urtuk, whose remaining orcs are being hunted by Gith warriors (*Adlishar Rrakhma*, led by Hurth and Rez'tha) purging the remnants of Saressess's mindspawn.
- Proving that Saressess is dead and the orcs are free of his control, the Keepers broker peace, earn the respect of the Gith, and bring together the "Brain Trust" (Tetrician, Ioryn, and Cendruil) to repair the crashed Gith airship *Mirha'Kith* in exchange for the Gith's aid in the coming war.

## Phase 6: Forging the Alliance

### Sessions 137-142
- Plane-shifting to the World Tree in the Feywild, the Keepers reunite with Vealas (Vea)—who has finished hunting down Shathras—and secure the support of the Council of the Four Seasons for the coming war against Shar.

## The Pickled Egg & Aribert
A shady tavern in Caer Callidyrr owned by the long-suffering Aribert, famous for his brutal "Aribert's Pickle Serum." The Keepers use it to unwind, much to the exasperated Aribert's alarm when they drag in magical creatures or cause chaos. They even casually partnered with him to mass-distribute branded "Crow's Eggs" (pickled eggs) across the islands. 

## Melito the Flustered Priest
An even-tempered elven priest at the Temple of Silvanus in Baldur's Gate. He is perpetually startled and flustered by the Keepers suddenly teleporting into his pristine temple—often battered, bruised, and bleeding from their latest near-death adventure—though he dutifully heals and defends them every time. He serves as their impromptu medical evacuation contact.

## Tetrician's Trauma
The fussy former royal tutor turned magical scholar at the Citadel. Brilliant and helpful (he crafts their anti-Shadowfell rings), he is nonetheless easily traumatized by the Keepers' casual violence—such as leaving dead bodies in his office—and lives in constant fear for his precious books when they visit.

## Nyx the Fae Dragon
A hyperactive, cute, but highly disruptive pet fae dragon. Nyx provides comedic relief by causing chaos in serious settings, like knocking over books in Tetrician's library and terrorizing the patrons of the Pickled Egg. He resides primarily in a magical lantern.

## The Clytin Claw Kobolds
Led by the surprisingly heroic Koldakask and his fierce mate Meldriss, this tribe of orange and mottled grey kobolds incorrectly revere blue dragon heritage. Formerly enslaved by a cruel black dragon the Keepers slew, they are now staunch, slightly comical allies of the Keepers and enthusiastically engage in "rat-hunting" (rabbit farming) and tunnel-digging.

## Prince Gregory's Rebellion
Despite being kidnapped and witnessing unspeakable horrors during the summoning of Kazgoroth, the 13-year-old Prince Gregory Kendrick is a rebellious brat who thinks highly of himself. The Keepers deal with his pompous attitude by dragging him to shady places like the Pickled Egg and getting him drunk to win his begrudging respect.

## Szeth's Bluntness
Szeth's complete lack of social tact usually manifests in situations requiring delicate diplomacy (e.g., confronting an assassin or a monarch by simply stating the bold, unvarnished truth).

## Aknemeia's Complicated Legacy
The lingering shadow of her father, Akmenos Mandorcai. He is not just evil; he is deeply interested in her growing power, occasionally feeding her information or manipulating events just to test her strength.

## Caryndal's Illusions & PR
Caryndal is the face of the group. Woven into the story must be his constant, sometimes overly-dramatic use of Fey magic (pixie fields, invisibility) not just for combat, but to actively manage the Keepers' public relations (e.g., spreading the word of their heroism after dismantling a slaver ring).

## Eryneth's Quiet Competence
Eryneth's skill contrasts with her own self-doubt.

## Lift and the Kooky Old Woman
The strange, unsettlingly funny subplot of Lift being tailed by a "kooky old woman" (who is actually the disguised, ancient silver dragon Kearastrix keeping tabs on them).

## Monster Parts
Szeth in particular is obsessed with collecting monster parts, especially dragon heads. This leads to the head of Casarioth being mounted on the wall of the Pickled Egg.
